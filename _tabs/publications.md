## 2024

<article class="publication-entry">
  <p>
    <strong>Bleichman, I.</strong>, Shefi, P., Kaminka, G., & Ayali, A.<br>
    <a href="https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae537"><em>The visual stimuli attributes instrumental for collective-motion-related decision-making in locusts.</em></a><br>
    <em>PNAS Nexus</em>
  </p>
  <div class="publication-summary">
    <details>
      <summary>Main findings</summary>
      <p>
        This study tested which visual features are most important for collective-motion-related decisions in locusts. The results indicate that response strength depends strongly on the number of moving objects and is further enhanced by vertical motion edges, while horizontal structure contributes to heading estimation and total stimulus area has a smaller effect on the behavioral response.
      </p>
    </details>
  </div>
</article>

<article class="publication-entry">
  <p>
    Aidan, Y.<sup>*</sup>, <strong>Bleichman, I.</strong><sup>*</sup>, & Ayali, A.<br>
    <a href="https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2023.0468"><em>Pausing to swarm: locust intermittent motion is instrumental for swarming-related visual processing.</em></a><br>
    <em>Biology Letters</em><br>
    <sup>*</sup>Equal contribution
  </p>
  <div class="publication-summary">
    <details>
      <summary>Main findings</summary>
      <p>
        This work shows that intermittent locomotion, and especially pause periods, is instrumental for swarming-related visual processing. Locusts respond more effectively to collective-motion cues when critical information is available during pauses, supporting the idea that stop phases are functionally important for visual sampling and collective decision-making.
      </p>
    </details>
  </div>
</article>

<article class="publication-entry">

    <strong>Bleichman, I.</strong>, Hiram-Bab, S., Gabet, Y., & Savion, N.<br>
    <a href="https://doi.org/10.3390/antiox13040474"><em>S-Allylmercapto-N-Acetylcysteine (ASSNAC) Attenuates Osteoporosis in Ovariectomized (OVX) Mice.</em></a><br>
    <em>Antioxidants</em>
  </p>
  <div class="publication-summary">
    <details>
      <summary>Main findings</summary>
      <p>
        In an ovariectomized mouse model of post-menopausal osteoporosis, ASSNAC treatment reduced bone-loss-related outcomes and improved bone-remodeling markers. The study also reports beneficial effects when ASSNAC is combined with alendronate, supporting antioxidant-based strategies as a complementary approach for osteoporosis management.
      </p>
    </details>

## 2023

<article class="publication-entry">
  <p>
    <strong>Bleichman, I.</strong>, Yadav, P., & Ayali, A.<br>
    <a href="https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2022.1862"><em>Visual processing and collective motion-related decision-making in desert locusts.</em></a><br>
    <em>Proceedings of the Royal Society B</em>
  </p>
  <div class="publication-summary">
    <details>
      <summary>Main findings</summary>
      <p>
        This study demonstrates that desert locusts selectively integrate socially relevant visual cues during collective-motion contexts. By combining controlled visual stimulation with behavioral and neurophysiological measurements, the work identifies feature-dependent weighting of visual inputs that supports swarm-relevant decision-making.
      </p>
    </details>
  </div>
</article>
